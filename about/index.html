What we do

Sudbury Water Damage Pros is a water damage and mold response service for homes and small businesses in Sudbury, Massachusetts, and five nearby towns. The 24/7 emergency line connects you to licensed and insured restoration professionals who handle extraction, drying, cleanup, mold control and rebuilding.

We keep the promises small and checkable. We explain the scope and price before work starts, we offer same-day response when available, and we serve Sudbury and nearby towns.

How a job runs

The dispatcher asks what happened, where the water is and whether anything electrical is wet. A technician then checks the water category, takes moisture readings and explains the plan. Equipment such as submersible pumps, air movers and LGR dehumidifiers is set up, and readings are logged on each visit.

When the structure meets its drying goal, the equipment comes out. Drying goals are set from readings taken in unaffected parts of the same house, so the target is your own building's normal, not a generic number. Each visit adds new readings to the log, and the log goes with the final paperwork. Cleanup, deodorizing and rebuild can continue with us or with your own contractor.

Standards we work to

Water damage work follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which defines water categories and drying practice. Mold work follows ANSI/IICRC S520. These are published industry standards, and you are free to ask any company whether its crews work to them.

We do not claim awards, review scores or customer counts. Those claims need proof, and a new local line should earn them honestly.

Local knowledge that changes the work

Sudbury has a river boundary with Wayland, Hop Brook running to the Sudbury River, and the Great Meadows and Assabet River wildlife refuge lands. Most houses use septic systems instead of town sewer. These facts change which questions we ask, from groundwater in a basement to whether a backup involves a tank.

Historic areas such as Sudbury Center and the Wayside Inn district add older buildings, and older construction can include plaster walls that dry differently from modern drywall.

How pricing and scope are handled

Cost depends on the water category, the affected area, the materials, the days of drying and the rebuild scope. Because those facts are only known on site, the plan and price are explained before work starts, and changes are discussed before they happen. Nothing on this site is a price quote, and we do not publish per-square-foot numbers.

If an insurance claim is involved, we keep photos, moisture readings and equipment logs. Your insurer decides coverage, and the Massachusetts Division of Insurance explains that standard homeowners policies do not cover flood damage.

Homes make up most of the calls, and small businesses along Route 20 are served too. Large commercial losses may need extra equipment or crews, which the dispatcher will explain.

What to do before help arrives

These steps are general safety guidance, not a promise about how a job will go. Do what is safe, and leave anything that is not.

  • Do not walk into standing water if outlets, cords or appliances are in it. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk.
  • Shut off the fixture valve or the main water valve to stop the source.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on water, and do not run ceiling fixtures under a sagging ceiling.
  • Keep children and pets out of any water that may be contaminated.
  • Take photos of the damage and the source before you move belongings.

What we do not do

We do not waterproof foundations, repair septic systems or fix plumbing. We do not promise insurance approval, direct billing or arrival times. If your situation needs another trade, we say so, and we point you to the right kind of contractor.
